How it works

One complaint, four accountable stages

Every step below is implemented in the running application. There are no simulated stages and no hidden manual edits.

Submit

Register or sign in, choose a category, describe the issue, pick the location on the map and attach optional evidence.

Classify

Gurugram text is classified with deterministic Indian keyword rules. The original text and the matched signals remain visible.

Route

Complaints are matched against the verified department directory. Ambiguous jurisdiction goes to manual administrative review with a written reason.

Resolve

Officers claim department-scoped work, update status with notes, and citizens follow the full history from their tracking ID.

Core capabilities

Built for accountability, not decoration

Each capability listed here is backed by an API contract and covered by the project test suite.

Deterministic classification

Gurugram complaints are classified by Indian keyword rules with recorded confidence and alternatives. The classifier is rule-based and inspectable.

Explainable priority

A transparent heuristic combines severity language, duplication and age. The reasons are stored with the complaint; the score is not a calibrated probability.

Verified routing

Departments come from a recorded directory with provenance. When no verified mapping exists the complaint stays unassigned instead of inventing a recipient.

Status history

Every transition is appended with actor, timestamp and comment. Citizens see the same history that officers and administrators rely on.

Protected evidence images

Uploads are validated for type and size, stored on the server, and served only through an authorised endpoint. Images are quality-checked, not AI-recognised.

Append-only audit log

Administrative actions such as manual routing and officer management are written to a paginated audit log that is not editable from the interface.

Data transparency

Three record types, never blended

UIMRS separates imported historical data, live citizen submissions and synthetic evaluation records. Every chart and every table states which type it uses.

Live UIMRS

Gurugram submissions

Filed by registered citizens through this application. Role-scoped: a citizen sees their own complaints, an officer sees their department, an administrator sees the municipality.

Historical — read-only

Bengaluru imported records

I Change My City / Janaagraha records imported for historical analysis. These rows are immutable in live workflows and are never counted as Gurugram model performance.

Evaluation only

Synthetic test records

Clearly labelled [EVALUATION TEST] records used to exercise workflows during evaluation. They are not municipal data and must not be read as real complaints.

ⓘ Note: public analytics cover historical records only. Live complaint counts, locations and identifiers are available only to authenticated officers and administrators.

Coverage and scope

Where UIMRS operates today

Gurugram, Haryana — primary city

Live complaints are submitted directly to UIMRS for Gurugram. Locations are validated against the configured municipal boundary before a complaint is accepted.

ⓘ There is no live MCG or GMDA API connection. Complaints are held in UIMRS and routed to recorded departments; they are not synchronised into a municipal system.

Bengaluru, Karnataka — historical only

Bengaluru appears only as imported, read-only historical data from I Change My City / Janaagraha. It is displayed on the public dashboard and in public analytics, clearly labelled as historical.

ⓘ Historical Bengaluru ML results are not Gurugram model performance, and Bengaluru records cannot be edited through live complaint workflows.

UIMRS is not a pan-India deployment. Coverage is limited to the cities described above, and no other city is claimed.

Future development

Proposed, not yet implemented

The three items below are planned extensions of UIMRS. None of them is operational in the current release.

Future / Planned

Real-Time Municipal Integration

Integrate with verified municipal corporation and government systems to synchronise complaint registration, routing and resolution status. Today no such connection exists.

Future / Planned

Multilingual Voice-Based Reporting

Allow citizens to submit civic complaints through voice input and multiple Indian languages, improving accessibility for users who prefer not to type.

Future / Planned

Mobile Application and Notifications

Develop dedicated Android and iOS applications with push notifications, complaint updates and convenient mobile access to the same UIMRS records.

ⓘ These features are shown for planning context only. No button on this page starts them, and no current screen implies that they are available.
