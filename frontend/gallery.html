Interactive 3D gallery

Nine civic issue categories, in three dimensions

Rotate, zoom and inspect a procedural 3D model for each UIMRS complaint category. The models are built in the browser from simple geometry and are locally hosted — no external model files are downloaded.

Illustrative only Not AI image recognition Not a digital twin Three.js · MIT licence

These models illustrate civic issue categories for the project presentation. They are not reconstructions of citizen complaints, not AI-generated scene understanding, and not a live digital twin of any city. Model geometry is built procedurally with three.js r160 (MIT).
